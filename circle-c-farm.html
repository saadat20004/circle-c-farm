Welcome to the farm

Close to everything.
Feels a world away.

Circle C Farm is a real, working 66-acre farm in Oregon's Willamette Valley: forest-covered hills, rolling hay fields and a small seasonal creek. The campsites sit nestled in the trees across the open hay field, the perfect spot to sit back and watch wildlife wander by at dusk.

There's a horse boarding stable, chickens and goats on the property. You'll see and hear some of the animals, but most are far enough away that they never disturb your rest. Ask your host Rachelle for a farm tour and she'll happily show you around.

Good to know

Open June – OctoberSites are seasonal and close for the wet winter months. Access may shift with the weather.
Watch for poison oakThere is some on the property, so please stay on the paths when exploring.
Barns & pasturesFor your safety, don't enter any barn or pasture without permission. Ask for a tour instead!
Access can varyAt times some sites are walk-in only (about 200 ft) or 4WD only, so nobody gets stuck.
It's a working farmNeighboring farm equipment may start early in the morning. It's part of the charm.
Private addressThe exact location is shared with you once your booking is confirmed.
