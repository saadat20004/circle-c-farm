Welcome to Circle C

Close to town.
Feels miles
from anywhere.

Just outside Yamhill, where routes 240 and 47 meet, our working farm rolls across forested hills and open hay fields. The camp sites sit tucked in the trees across the field, where you can watch deer and birds come out at dusk.

Pick blackberries, apples and plums in season. Say hello to the horses, chickens and goats on a farm tour. Then sit by the fire and let the stars come out.

Before you go

Good to know

A few quick notes so your stay goes smoothly.

When are you open?

The sites are seasonal, open from June through October. They close for the wet winter months, and access may shift with the weather.

Where exactly is the farm?

About 3 miles outside Yamhill, Oregon, near where routes 240 and 47 meet. The exact address is shared after your booking is confirmed.

Can I drive right to my site?

Usually. At times some sites are walk-in only (about a 200 ft walk) or 4WD only, because they sit in a low part of the farm.

Can we see the animals?

Yes! Message Rachelle for a farm tour of the horse stable, chickens and goats. For your safety, please don't enter barns or pastures without permission.

Anything to watch out for?

There is some poison oak on the property, so please stay on the paths when you explore the fields.

Is it quiet?

Very. The animals are far enough away not to disturb you, though a neighbor's farm equipment may start early some mornings.
